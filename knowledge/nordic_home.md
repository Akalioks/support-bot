# Nordic Home — customer FAQ

Nordic Home is an online store of Scandinavian-style home goods: textiles, lamps, kitchenware and small furniture. Online orders only; pick-up point in Tallinn.

## Contacts
- Email: hello@nordichome.example
- Phone: +372 600 0000 (Mon–Fri 9:00–18:00)
- Pick-up point: Telliskivi 60, Tallinn (Mon–Sat 11:00–19:00)

## Delivery (Estonia)
- Tallinn: next working day, €3.90.
- Tartu, Pärnu and the rest of Estonia: 1–2 working days, €4.90.
- Free delivery for orders over €50.
- Parcel lockers (Omniva, DPD, SmartPost): €2.90, 1–2 working days.
- Pick-up from Telliskivi 60: free, ready in 2 hours on working days.
- Orders placed before 14:00 on a working day ship the same day.

## Delivery (abroad)
- Latvia and Lithuania: 2–4 working days, €7.90; free over €80.
- Finland: 2–4 working days, €9.90; free over €100.
- Other EU countries: 4–7 working days, €14.90.
- We don't ship outside the EU.

## Payment
- Bank link (Swedbank, SEB, LHV, Luminor, Coop), Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, Google Pay.
- Pay later in 3 interest-free parts with Esto for orders from €60.
- Prices include 24% VAT. Invoices for companies on request by email.

## Returns and exchanges
- 30 days to return unused items in original packaging, no reason needed.
- Return shipping in Estonia is free via any parcel locker — the return code is in the order email.
- Refunds are made to the original payment method within 5 working days after we receive the item.
- Personalised items (engraved boards, custom-size curtains) can't be returned unless faulty.
- Damaged on arrival: send a photo to hello@nordichome.example within 7 days, we replace it for free.

## Warranty
- 2 years on all products under EU law; 5 years on our own-brand "Nordic Home Studio" furniture.

## Product care
- Linen bedding: wash at 40°C, don't tumble dry on high heat; it softens after a few washes.
- Wool throws: hand wash cold or dry clean; air dry flat.
- Oak boards: hand wash only, oil monthly with food-safe oil.
- Ceramic tableware: dishwasher and microwave safe unless marked "hand painted".

## Orders and account
- You can change or cancel an order within 1 hour of placing it — contact us by phone or email.
- Order status and tracking link are in the confirmation email. For a specific order, a manager can check it.
- Gift cards: €25, €50, €100; valid 2 years; sent by email.
